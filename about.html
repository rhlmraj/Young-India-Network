Young India Network

Young India Network

is a student-led community that brings together students from IITs, NITs, IIITs, IISERs, IISc, IIMs and other Centrally Funded Technical Institutions (CFTIs).

We want to create a space where students from different campuses can connect, exchange ideas, learn from one another and work together. Our aim is to encourage scientific thinking, explore new ideas and use our collective knowledge and skills to address challenges facing society.

Beyond academics and technology, we also believe in the importance of student well-being, mental health and supporting one another. We hope to build an inclusive community where students feel heard, valued and encouraged to engage in meaningful conversations.
